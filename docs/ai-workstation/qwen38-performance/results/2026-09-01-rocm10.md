# E003 Results: Stable ROCm 10

Date: 2026-09-01 (America/Chicago)

## Variable Under Test

The exact Q4_K_M model and E002 server flags were moved from the existing ROCm
7.14 toolbox to a side-by-side current stable ROCm 10 toolbox.

Post-test idle evidence for ROCm 10 was 0% GPU use, 49 C, about 11.4 W socket
graphics package power, and 22.2 GB decimal GPU memory used.

## Decision

Treat ROCm 10 as neutral. The candidate is healthy and slightly better in some
samples, but the difference is too small and inconsistent to justify replacing
the known-working service. Retain the toolbox for later compatibility and
combined-candidate testing after independent variables are complete.
